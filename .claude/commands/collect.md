---
description: Fetch financial data and news context for a ticker, or macro market data (first step of any analysis)
argument-hint: "<TICKER> | --macro"
---

Use the collect-data skill with these arguments: $ARGUMENTS

Examples: `/collect FPT` collects all ticker data (financials + news context + annual report); `/collect --macro` collects macro market data for the weekly report.

If no arguments were provided, ask whether to collect a specific ticker or macro data.
