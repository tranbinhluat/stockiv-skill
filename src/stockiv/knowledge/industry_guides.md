# Industry Analysis Guides (Vietnam Specific)

> **🧠 CORE RESOURCE — For Synthesizer and Quant Analyst Crews**
> *Use these metrics to classify and evaluate companies within their respective industries.*

## 1. Banking (Ngân hàng)

**Characteristics:** Sustainable Growth. Heavily regulated. High leverage is normal; debt/equity metrics do not apply standardly.

**Key Metrics (LLM Thresholds):**

| Metric | Description | Excellent | Good | Fair | Poor |
|--------|-------------|-----------|------|------|------|
| **NIM** (Net Interest Margin) | Profitability of core lending | > 4.0% | 3.5% - 4.0% | 2.5% - 3.5% | < 2.5% |
| **NPL** (Non-Performing Loans) | Asset quality (Lower is better) | < 1.0% | 1.0% - 1.5% | 1.5% - 2.5% | > 2.5% |
| **CAR** (Capital Adequacy Ratio) | Safety buffer | > 15% | 12% - 15% | 9% - 12% | < 9% |
| **CASA** (Current Account Savings) | Cheap funding source | > 40% | 30% - 40% | 20% - 30% | < 20% |

**Vietnam Context:** Credit growth quotas set by SBV determine growth ceilings. Retail banks usually have higher NIM and CASA than corporate banks.

---

## 2. Real Estate (Bất động sản)

**Characteristics:** Cyclical. Highly dependent on credit availability, interest rates, and legal approvals.

**Key Metrics (LLM Thresholds):**

| Metric | Description | Excellent | Good | Fair | Poor |
|--------|-------------|-----------|------|------|------|
| **Inventory Turnover** | Speed of selling projects | < 1 year | 1 - 2 years | 2 - 3 years | > 3 years |
| **Debt/Asset Ratio** | Leverage risk | < 20% | 20% - 30% | 30% - 50% | > 50% |
| **Pre-sales / Revenue** | Future revenue visibility | > 50% | 30% - 50% | 10% - 30% | < 10% |

**Vietnam Context:** Look for "Người mua trả tiền trước" (Customer advances) on the balance sheet. This is unearned revenue and a highly positive signal. 

---

## 3. Technology (Công nghệ)

**Characteristics:** Fast Growth. Asset-light. Growth requires continuous R&D.

**Key Metrics (LLM Thresholds):**

| Metric | Description | Excellent | Good | Fair | Poor |
|--------|-------------|-----------|------|------|------|
| **Revenue Growth** | Top-line expansion | > 30% | 20% - 30% | 10% - 20% | < 10% |
| **Gross Margin** | Pricing power | > 40% | 30% - 40% | 20% - 30% | < 20% |
| **R&D / Revenue** | Future innovation fuel | > 15% | 10% - 15% | 5% - 10% | < 5% |

**Vietnam Context:** Software outsourcing (e.g., FPT) depends heavily on foreign exchange rates (JPY, USD). Domestic software depends on digital transformation budgets.

---

## 4. Construction & Materials (Xây dựng & Vật liệu)

**Characteristics:** Cyclical. Highly sensitive to public investment (FDI/infrastructure) and the Real Estate cycle.

**Key Metrics (LLM Thresholds):**

| Metric | Description | Excellent | Good | Fair | Poor |
|--------|-------------|-----------|------|------|------|
| **Order Book / Rev** | Remaining contracts | > 2.0x | 1.5x - 2.0x | 1.0x - 1.5x | < 1.0x |
| **Days Sales Outstanding** | Payment collection speed | < 45 days | 45 - 90 days | 90 - 180 days | > 180 days |
| **EBITDA Margin** | Operating profitability | > 15% | 10% - 15% | 5% - 10% | < 5% |

**Vietnam Context:** High risk of "Phải thu" (Receivables) becoming bad debt. If Accounts Receivable grows exponentially faster than Revenue, trigger an anomaly.

---

## 5. Logistics & Ports (Ngành Cảng & Vận tải)

**Characteristics:** Cyclical/Sustainable depending on the exact niche. Relies on trade volumes (FDI exports/imports).

**Key Metrics (LLM Thresholds):**

| Metric | Description | Excellent | Good | Fair | Poor |
|--------|-------------|-----------|------|------|------|
| **Fleet/Port Utilization** | Operating efficiency | > 85% | 75% - 85% | 60% - 75% | < 60% |
| **Debt / Equity** | Leverage | < 0.5x | 0.5x - 1.0x | 1.0x - 1.5x | > 1.5x |
| **Operating Margin** | Profitability | > 20% | 15% - 20% | 10% - 15% | < 10% |

**Vietnam Context:** Highly exposed to global shipping rates (e.g., Baltic Dry Index). Look out for fuel price fluctuations.

---

## 6. Insurance (Bảo hiểm)

**Characteristics:** Sustainable Growth/Defensive. Profits come from underwriting (Float) AND investment of the float.

**Key Metrics (LLM Thresholds):**

| Metric | Description | Excellent | Good | Fair | Poor |
|--------|-------------|-----------|------|------|------|
| **Combined Ratio** | Claims + Expenses / Premium | < 95% | 95% - 100% | 100% - 105% | > 105% |
| **Investment Yield** | Return on float | > % Deposit | Average Deposit | Low | Negative |
| **Premium Growth** | Growth rate | > 15% | 10% - 15% | 5% - 10% | < 5% |

**Vietnam Context:** Non-life insurers rely heavily on bank deposit rates, meaning high interest rates generally boost their bottom lines quickly.

---

## 7. Telecommunications (Viễn thông)

**Characteristics:** Slow Growth/Defensive. High barrier to entry, capital intensive initially, but massive free cash flow later.

**Key Metrics (LLM Thresholds):**

| Metric | Description | Excellent | Good | Fair | Poor |
|--------|-------------|-----------|------|------|------|
| **ARPU** | Average Rev Per User | High Growth | Slow Growth | Flat | Declining |
| **Churn Rate** | Customer loss | < 1.0% | 1.0% - 2.0% | 2.0% - 5.0% | > 5.0% |
| **EBITDA Margin** | Cash generation | > 45% | 35% - 45% | 25% - 35% | < 25% |

**Vietnam Context:** Often extremely stable dividend payers due to saturated market. Watch for heavy CAPEX cycles (e.g., 5G rollout) which may temporarily depress Free Cash Flow.

---

## 8. Consumer Goods & Retail (Tiêu dùng & Bán lẻ)

**Characteristics:** Sustainable/Fast Growth. Depends on consumer purchasing power and distribution network.

**Key Metrics (LLM Thresholds):**

| Metric | Description | Excellent | Good | Fair | Poor |
|--------|-------------|-----------|------|------|------|
| **Inventory Turnover** | Speed of selling goods | > 12x | 8x - 12x | 4x - 8x | < 4x |
| **Gross Margin** | Brand/Pricing power | > 50% | 30% - 50% | 15% - 30% | < 15% |
| **Same Store Sales** | Growth of existing stores | > 10% | 5% - 10% | 0% - 5% | Negative |

**Vietnam Context:** Watch out for "expansion trap" where revenues grow via opening new stores but Same Store Sales Growth (SSSG) is negative and debt piles up.
