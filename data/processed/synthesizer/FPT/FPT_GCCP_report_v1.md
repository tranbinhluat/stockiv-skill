### Industry Dynamics & Lynch Category

FPT operates in the **Technology** sector, which is classified as **Fast Growth** according to the GCCP framework. The company exhibits key characteristics of this category: significant top-line expansion (targeting 30% revenue growth in 2026 [[2]](https://www.vneconomy.vn/fpt-targets-30-percent-revenue-growth-in-2026-despite-global-headwinds-2026-03-08)), a strategic focus on high-margin future technologies like Generative AI [[4]](https://www.vnreview.vn/fpt-launches-ai-research-center-in-hanoi-2026-focus-on-genai-technologies), and continuous investment in R&D and innovation.

Based on its profile, FPT is classified as a **Fast Grower** under Peter Lynch's categories. #### Quantitative Anomalies
While the overall picture is positive, two key areas warrant attention:
1.  **Gross Margin Compression:** There is a persistent, multi-year trend of gross margin compression, declining from 39.01% in 2022 to 36.93% in 2025 [Financial Summary](/home/luattranb/Documents/projects/stock-agent/data/processed/synthesizer/FPT/FPT_financial_summary.md). However, this has been more than offset by operational efficiency, as net profit margin has expanded.
2.  **Working Capital Bloat:** In 2025, Accounts Receivable grew at 20.9%, nearly twice the rate of revenue growth (11.6%) [Financial Summary](/home/luattranb/Documents/projects/stock-agent/data/processed/synthesizer/FPT/FPT_financial_summary.md). This could indicate a loosening of credit terms to boost sales or potential collection issues and should be monitored closely.
